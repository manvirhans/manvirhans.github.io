The Hans Family Dashboard

Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Who uses the app

The Hans Family Dashboard is a private app for the members of one family. Accounts exist only by invitation from the family; there is no public sign-up.

What Google data the app reads

When a family member connects their Google account, the app asks for two read-only permissions:

  • See and download your Google Drive files (drive.readonly), to list the folders the family has chosen and the files in them.
  • See your Google Sheets spreadsheets (spreadsheets.readonly), to read the rows of the spreadsheets the family has chosen.

The app never creates, changes, moves, shares or deletes anything in Google Drive or Google Sheets.

How it is used and where it is kept

  • Data read from Google is stored only in a database on the family's own computer, and is shown only to the family members who have been given access to it.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept on that same computer, readable only by the account that runs the app.
  • Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone outside the family, not used for advertising, and not sent to analytics or AI services.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Hans Family Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is withdrawn the app can no longer read anything, and the family can delete the copies stored on their computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the family member who invited you to the app.
